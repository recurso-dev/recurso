# Recurso — Brand Voice

> Keeps every word — UI copy, docs, marketing — consistent. When writing any
> user-facing text, this doc wins over instinct.

## Voice

Calm · confident · precise · technical · trustworthy. We sound like a finance
tool that has already thought about the edge case you're worried about.

Never salesy. Never exaggerated. Never breathless. We are talking to someone who
will be audited on the numbers we produce — earn their trust by being exact, not
by being loud.

## Words to avoid

World-class · revolutionary · best-in-class · cutting-edge · game-changing ·
seamless · effortless · powerful · unleash · supercharge · next-generation ·
AI-powered (as a selling point) · "just" (as in "just click here").

Hype words make a finance buyer trust us *less*. They read them as a substitute
for substance.

## Words to use

Auditable · reliable · accurate · deterministic · reproducible · transparent ·
reconciled · traceable · reversible · exact. These are claims we can back with
the ledger.

## How we write

- **Short sentences.** State the fact, then stop.
- **Explain, don't hype.** "Every invoice posts a double-entry ledger leg" beats
  "powerful accounting engine."
- **Name things by what the user recognizes**, not how the system is built. A
  person manages *notifications*, not *webhook config*.
- **Active voice.** A control says exactly what happens ("Publish", then a toast
  that says "Published").
- **Numbers are specific.** "$34,525.00 past due across 7 invoices," not "some
  overdue invoices."
- **Errors explain what went wrong and how to fix it** — no apologies, no
  "Unknown error," no vagueness. (See `ANTI_PATTERNS.md`.)

## Copy examples

| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| "Something went wrong" | "We couldn't reach the payment gateway. Retry, or check the connection in Settings." |
| "Powerful revenue recognition" | "Deferred revenue recognizes on schedule; the month-end close proves it ties out." |
| "Unexplained delta −$26,854" | "$26,854 to reconcile — recognition schedules build at payment." |
| "Are you sure?" | "Void invoice INV-1043? This can't be undone." |
